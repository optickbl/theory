# Theory

A personal trainer for the Greek driving theory test (category B), in Greek and English.

**Live:** https://optickbl.github.io/theory/. In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

- **Mock exam** in the real format: 30 questions, one from each of the 30 question groups the exam draws from, 35 minutes, pass with at most 1 mistake.
- **Signs**: every sign and road-marking question in the bank, as flashcards or a quiz.
- **Practice by topic**, **mistakes review** and **smart practice** across all 825 questions.
- Works offline once opened.

Questions, answers and pictures come from the Ministry of Transport's TestDrive question
bank (Greek and English), filtered to the 825-question category B structure. Some rules have
changed since that bank was written (Law 5209/2025); those questions show a "Rule update"
note with the current rule. This is an unofficial study aid, not the live exam database.
